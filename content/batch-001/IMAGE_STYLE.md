# TripCaution — Image style for Batch 001 (reference-locked)

All **200 image prompts** in `image-manifest.csv`, `image-manifest.json`, and the corresponding **100 hero prompts** in `articles.json` share the identical style prefix below. This recreates the visual character of the user-provided TripCaution airport-security editorial illustration while giving each guide a distinct, scene-specific subject.

## Mandatory style prefix

> Moody editorial travel-safety illustration in the TripCaution reference style: semi-realistic hand-painted digital gouache and ink-wash, confident charcoal keylines, layered matte brushwork, atmospheric cinematic lighting, slightly distressed off-white paper texture. Restrained cool slate-blue, steel gray, near-black and deep midnight navy; small muted brick-red caution accents and selective warm amber light. An irregular dark illustrated scene framed by off-white paper margins, sharp layered angular navy/red chevrons and broken directional warning motifs around the edges, like a striking graphic-novel editorial print. Adult travelers with believable human anatomy and expressive but understated faces, strong readable environmental storytelling. Stylized painting, not a photograph, not flat vector, not anime, not cute cartoon, not glossy 3D.

## Composition

- **Hero / thumbnail (odd slots)**: medium-wide narrative scene, clear traveler decision, relevant environment, bold layered midnight-navy and muted-brick-red angular warning motifs, irregular inked scene panel on off-white paper.
- **Inline (even slots)**: tighter over-the-shoulder or detail-focused practical verification scene. Keep the same painterly palette, paper and decorative caution frame, but never repeat the hero camera angle.
- Content must be *plausible and location-aware* without fabricating a real event, government logo, sign, officer uniform, travel rule or dangerous situation.
- For Myanmar, use planning/advisory scenes only. Never portray a positive touring itinerary while official warnings require caution.
- Absolutely no embedded lettering, headings, watermark, stock-photo look, 3D or anime.
