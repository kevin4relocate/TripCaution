# Batch 003 illustration style and watermark

All **200 images** share the same core editorial illustration art direction:

> Moody editorial travel-safety illustration in the TripCaution reference style

### Watermark — required on every hero and inline image
The exact lowercase domain **`tripcaution.com`** should appear once, in legible dark-navy letters near the lower center of the off-white paper border, inset to survive cropping. Avoid other words, labels or commercial marks.

Each prompt embeds the full style and watermark wording, not an external suffix. Hero images use wider narrative scenes; inline images use closer practical-check perspectives. Do not invent official signs, uniforms, fake risk incidents or visually imply unavailable services.

Image AI may misspell the watermark. Inspect all rendered assets; when necessary, add the text deterministically in your image-editing stage rather than accepting a misspelled mark.

### Image naming
`Gen_001.webp`–`Gen_200.webp` always restart **within** `Images/Batch-003/`. R2 filenames are **unique SEO slugs** from the manifest, not repeated input names. Captions and alt tags are provisional until the finished images have been reviewed.
