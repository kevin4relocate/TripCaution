# Batch 004 illustration rules

All 200 prompts use the existing TripCaution reference style: painterly semi-realistic gouache and ink wash, charcoal outlines, off-white paper, midnight navy/slate-blue shadows and muted brick-red highlights. Hero scenes are medium-wide with a human decision and locally appropriate setting; inline scenes are close views of the actual check or object.

Each independent prompt includes the exact lowercase visible `tripcaution.com` watermark, dark navy near the lower-center ivory-paper border, inset for safe cropping. Do not accept misspelled generated text; correct it in the image-editing stage if needed. No other readable lettering, logos, fake official signage, sensational incidents, 3D, photography or flat vector style.

`Gen_001.webp` and `Gen_002.webp` are hero and inline for guide 1, through `Gen_199.webp` and `Gen_200.webp` for guide 100. Upload to `Images/Batch-004` only. Never reuse prior R2 filenames: the manifest's slug-based objects are unique.
